## Project Overview

Welcome to the **DP Physics Bridge Program** portal. 

IB Diploma Programme (DP) Physics is widely regarded as one of the most rigorous pre-university physics courses in the world. The barrier to success is rarely the physics concepts themselves; instead, students frequently struggle with the **fluency of mathematical application**, **dimensional analysis**, and **precision tracking**.

This platform is a 12-week framework engineered to eliminate that friction. By mastering these foundational toolkits before your official syllabus begins, you shift your cognitive load away from battling algebra and toward genuinely understanding the mechanics of the universe.

---

## Strategic Objectives

* **Zero-Lag Algebra & Geometry:** Develop instinctual fluency in rearranging complex physical formulas and manipulating vector spaces.
* **Dimensional Fluency:** Move past memorizing units to using dimensional consistency as a diagnostic tool for error-checking equations.
* **Scientific Precision:** Standardize measurement logging, scientific notation conversions, and strict significant figure execution to protect easy exam marks.
* **Intuitive Problem Solving:** Transition from passive formula-hunting to active, structured physical modeling.

---

## How to Navigate This Course

1.  **Daily Micro-Practice:** Use the left sidebar to navigate through the weekly modules. Each day introduces a hyper-focused problem set.
2.  **Paper & Pen Execution:** Treat these problems as official exam training. Work through derivations step-by-step on paper before checking your logic.
3.  **Core Reference Material:** Utilize the `Reference` directory in the sidebar for consolidated data booklets, unit prefixes, and fundamental constants.

> "Physics is structured grammar; mathematics is the language. You cannot write poetry about the cosmos until you master the alphabet."
