## Project Overview

Welcome to the **DP Bridge Platform Portal**. 

The International Baccalaureate (IB) Diploma Programme courses in Higher Level Mathematics (Analysis & Approaches) and Physics are widely recognized as some of the most rigorous pre-university sequences in the world. The barrier to high-tier performance ($7s$) is rarely a student's lack of logical capacity; instead, data shows that students routinely struggle due to **foundational algebraic degradation**, **notation anxiety**, and **over-reliance on calculators**.

This platform hosts two independent, 8 week Math and 12-week Physics frameworks engineered to eliminate that friction. By mastering these core execution mechanisms *before* the rapid pace of the official syllabus begins, students shift their cognitive load away from battling basic manipulation and toward deep analytical reasoning.

## What We Cover: The 8-Week Diagnostic Alignment

### 📐 Mathematics Analysis & Approaches (HL)
* **Week 1: The Foundation Matrix & Systems** — Solving $3 \times 3$ multi-variable linear systems, mastering coordinate bisectors, point-slope parameters, and space transformation machines ($2 \times 2$ matrices).
* **Week 2: Functions, Domains, & Inverse Rigor** — Transitioning from simple coordinate mapping to formal injection restrictions, composite chains, asymptotic boundaries, and self-inverse structures.
* **Weeks 3–8: Advanced Runways** — Developing foundational runways across trigonometry, polynomial analysis, calculus limit definitions, and vector geometries.

## What We Cover: The 12-Week Diagnostic Alignment
### ⚛️ DP Physics (HL)
* **Week 1: Mathematical Fluency & Subject Manipulation** — Rearranging complex literal equations, literal variable cancellations, and reciprocal proportions.
* **Week 2: Dimensional Analysis & Scientific Formatting** — Mastering metric prefixes, unit conversions, and verifying physical formulas using base SI dimensions.
* **Weeks 3–12: Applied Engineering** — Vector resolution, scalar dot products, uncertainty propagation, and parsing data booklets.
